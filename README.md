# Air Pollution and Power Outage Severity

**Research question:** Does air pollution predict how severe a power outage will be within the United States?

Project done for COGS 108 — Data Science in Practice at UCSD.

Manning Karahashi, Andrew Gooboian, Emily Nguyen, Lucas Vocelka and Christian Paddie are responsible for the creation of this project.

My contribution: background research and literature synthesis, project conceptualization and framing of the research question, and co-production of the summary video.

[Watch our 5-minute project video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u7ezw58YGtM)

## Data

- [CACES U.S. Air Quality](https://www.caces.us/data)
- [Purdue Major Power Outage Dataset](https://engineering.purdue.edu/LASCI/research-data/outages)
